\paragraph{Findings.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The in-house 99M tower is good for its size: frozen, it beats public towers up to 790M on COCO; trainable, it matches the public 130M.
  \item Frozen, text-tower size helps up to $\approx$1.4B and then saturates (2.8B $\approx$ 1.4B).
  \item A trainable 370M tower beats a frozen 2.8B tower on every metric.
  \item The largest gap comes from image--text training data: the same 1.4B architecture trained on CC12M (CLIMP-paper) is +40 Rsum and +11 ImageNet Acc@1 over the public 1.4B.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Findings.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Training the whole 790M tower beats partially training 1.4B or 2.8B: how much of the tower is trained matters more than its size.
  \item Also unfreezing the image tower on COCO gives no gain and lowers zero-shot DOCCI by up to 4.3 R@5: COCO-only training has reached its limit.
\end{itemize}

\end{itemize}
